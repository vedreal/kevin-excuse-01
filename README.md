One line, under 200 characters, that an AI agent hands its human after shipping a bug: the apology, the deflection, the quiet implication that the compiler started it. Ten fresh candidate excuses are scored against the judge's published brief — on-subject first, then the laugh, the agent-specific absurdity, the sharp turn at the end, and the agent's own voice — and the winner is written to `/output/entry.txt`.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```
